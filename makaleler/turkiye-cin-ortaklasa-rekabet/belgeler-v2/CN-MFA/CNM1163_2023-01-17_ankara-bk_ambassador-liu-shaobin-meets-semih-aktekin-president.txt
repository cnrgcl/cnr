Ambassador Liu Shaobin Meets Semih Aktekin  President of Nevşehir Hacı Bektaş Veli University

On July 7, 2022, Ambassador Liu Shaobin met with Mr. Semih Aktekin, President of Nevşehir Hacı Bektaş Veli University of Türkiye. The two sides exchanged views on strengthening educational and cultural exchanges between China and Türkiye.

Ambassador Liu said that both China and Türkiye are ancient civilizations which have made positive contributions to the progress of world civilization. Over the past over 50 years since the establishment of diplomatic relations, the educational and cultural exchanges between the two countries have been continuously strengthened, which has effectively enhanced our mutual understanding and friendship. President Aktekin and Nevşehir University have been promoting language education and cultural exchanges between China and Türkiye throughout these years. The Chinese Department was established, attracting many Chinese language lovers. They actively participated in the Chinese Bridge (Hanyu Qiao) Chinese Proficiency Competition and achieved good results. The Chinese side is willing to increase cooperation with Nevşehir University and promote educational and cultural exchanges between the two countries, so as to further strengthen the bond between our two peoples and contribute to China-Türkiye friendship.

President Aktekin stated that he attaches great importance to educational and cultural cooperation with China. He sincerely hopes to further promote cooperation between Nevşehir  University and the Chinese side in the areas of language and culture, scholar and overseas students exchange programs, and training of tourism professionals. Nevşehir University is willing to serve as a window and bridge for the friendship between China and Türkiye, so that the seeds of friendship will take root and blossom in Türkiye. Aktekin thanked the Chinese Embassy for its support to Chinese language teaching in the university.
