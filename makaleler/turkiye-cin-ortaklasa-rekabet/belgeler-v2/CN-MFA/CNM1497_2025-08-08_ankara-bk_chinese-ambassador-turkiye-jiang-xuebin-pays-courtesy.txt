Chinese Ambassador to Türkiye Jiang Xuebin Pays a Courtesy Call on Minister of Trade Ömer Bolat

On August 5th, Chinese Ambassador to Türkiye Jiang Xuebin paid a courtesy call on Minister of Trade Ömer Bolat. The two sides exchanged views on deepening China-Türkiye relations and trade cooperation. Deputy Minister of Trade Mustafa Tuzcu also attended the meeting.
Ambassador Jiang stated that under the strategic guidance of the two heads of state, China-Türkiye relations have maintained steady momentum, with positive progress made in such practical cooperation as trade across various fields. China is ready to strengthen communication with Türkiye, make full use of various cooperation mechanisms such as the China-Türkiye Joint Economic and Trade Committee, expand the breadth and depth of cooperation, and achieve a higher level of mutual benefit and win-win results.
Bolat stated that Türkiye and China enjoy a long-standing friendship. Türkiye looks forward to further deepening cooperation with China in the fields of economy, trade, investment, and tourism. The current global trade system is facing major challenges. It is hoped that China, as an economic powerhouse and a major trading nation, will play a greater role in promoting global trade.
