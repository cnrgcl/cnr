Ambassador Liu Shaobin meets Mr. Kemal Baytaş President of Türkiye-China Friendship Association

On May 19, 2021, Ambassador Liu Shaobin met with Mr. Kemal Baytaş, President of Türkiye-China Friendship Association. The two sides exchanged views on commemoration of the 50th anniversary of diplomatic relations between China and Türkiye and promotion of people-to-people and cultural exchanges between the two countries.
