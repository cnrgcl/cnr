关于通过中国签证申请服务中心申办中国香港/澳门签证的通知
Notice on applications for Hong Kong/Macau SAR Visas via the Chinese Visa Application Service Center

为向中国香港/澳门签证申请人提供舒适的办证环境和优质服务，自2019年5月1日起，外国公民申请中国香港/澳门普通签证，需到中国签证申请服务中心递交申请，中国驻伊斯坦布尔总领馆不再直接受理上述签证申请。具体安排如下：
一、中国签证申请服务中心联系方式
（一）地址： Maslak Mah. Meydan Sokak. No.5 Spring Giz Plaza, Kat:14 D47-48, 34398 Sariyer/Istanbul
（二）电话：+90（212）2760701
（三）传真：+90（212）2760702
（四）电子邮箱：istanbulcenter@visaforchina.org
（五）网址：www.visaforchina.org
二、办证时间及费用
（一）工作时间：周一至周五（节假日除外）
（二）受理时间：09:00-12:00
（三）收费和取证时间: 09:00-12:00，13:00-15:00
（四）费用收取：中国签证申请服务中心除代我馆收取签证规费、急件费和联络费外，将另行收取一定数额的服务费，具体收费标准请径询签证中心或参考其网站。
（五）审理时限：普通件，通常在使领馆正式受理后第7个工作日可取证。加急服务须经领事官员批准，并将加收费用。基于个案情况不同，部分申请需转递香港特区政府或澳门特区政府审批，故审理时间不确定。
三、特别提示
（一） 能否颁发签证由总领馆决定，中国签证申请服务中心不参与审批程序。
（二）中国香港/澳门签证申请人无需在线填表和采集指纹。
（三）申请人可通过中国签证服务中心向总领馆申请中国香港/澳门签证，亦可自行向香港特区政府入境事务处或澳门特区政府治安警察局出入境事务厅申请。
（四）关于免办港澳签证的情形请查阅香港特区政府入境事务处官方网站或澳门特区政府治安警察局出入境事务厅官方网站。
如果对签证申请服务中心所提供服务有任何改进意见和建议，欢迎与中国驻伊斯坦布尔总领馆联系。电子邮箱：istanbul@csm.mfa.gov.cn

中国驻伊斯坦布尔总领馆
2019年4月28日

In order to provide better visa application services, as of 1 May 2019, applicants who hold ordinary passports and apply for ordinary visas for Hong Kong/Macau SAR are required to submit their applications at the Chinese Visa Application Service Center. The Consulate-General of the People’s Republic of China in Istanbul will no longer accept such visa applications. The detailed arrangements are as follows:

1. Contact details for the Chinese Visa Application Service Center
(1) Address : Maslak Mah. Meydan Sokak. No.5 Spring Giz Plaza，Kat:14 D47-48，34398  Sariyer / Istanbul
(2) Tel : +90（212）2760701
(3) Fax : +90（212）2760702
(4) Email : istanbulcenter@visaforchina.org
(5) Official Website: www.visaforchina.org

2. Processing time and Payment
(1) Working days: Monday to Friday (exc. Holidays)
(2) Application submission: 09:00-12:00
(3) Payment and Collection Time: 09:00-12:00，13:00-15:00
(4) Fee: The Chinese Visa Application Service Center will charge visa fees, express service fees and liaison fees on behalf of the Consulate-General. Extra service fees will also be charged. For more information, please ask the Chinese Visa Application Service Center or visit its official websites.
(5) Processing Time: Visa collection can be as early as 7 working days after the submission for standard visa applications. Express service need to be approved by Consular Officer, and extra fees will be charged. Due to different cases, some applications need to be forwarded to Hong Kong Immigration Department or Macau Public Security Police Force, therefore the processing time is uncertain.

3. Additional notes
(1) The Chinese Visa Application Service Center is only responsible for the handling of the application submissions. The Consulate-General of the People’s Republic of China in Istanbul is in charge of the process of decision-making and visa issuing.
(2) Applicants who apply for visas for Hong Kong/Macau SAR, are not required to fill the online application forms, pre-book appointment time slots or have their biometrics information collected.
(3) Applicants can either choose to submit applications via Chinese Visa Application Service Center, or to Hong Kong Immigration Department or Macau Public Security Police Force directly.
(4) For visa-exemption arrangement, please visit the official website of Hong Kong Immigration Department or Macau Public Security Police Force .

If you would like to provide feedbacks or suggestions for the services of Chinese Visa Application Service Center, you are welcome to contact the Consulate-General of the People’s Republic of China in Istanbul.
Email: istanbul@csm.mfa.gov.cn

The Consulate-General of the People’s Republic of China in Istanbul
