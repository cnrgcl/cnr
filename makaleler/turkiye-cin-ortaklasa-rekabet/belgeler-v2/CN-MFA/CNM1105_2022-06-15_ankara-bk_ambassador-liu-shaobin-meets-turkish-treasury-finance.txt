Ambassador Liu Shaobin Meets Turkish Treasury and Finance Minister Lütfi Elvan

On August 31, 2021, Ambassador Liu Shaobin met with Mr. Lütfi Elvan, Minister of Treasury and Finance of Türkiye. The two sides exchanged views on the mechanism of the China-Türkiye Inter-governmental Cooperation Committee as well as bilateral cooperation in the fields of economy, trade, tourism and finance.
