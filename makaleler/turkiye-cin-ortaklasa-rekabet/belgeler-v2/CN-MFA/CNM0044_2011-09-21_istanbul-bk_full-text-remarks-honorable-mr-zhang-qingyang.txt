Full Text：Remarks by Honorable Mr. ZHANG Qingyang, Consul General of the People’s Republic of China in Istanbul, at the Reception Celebrating the 62nd Anniversary of the Founding of the People’s Republic of China

Honorable Mr. ZHANG Qingyang, Consul General of the People’s Republic of China in Istanbul, held the Reception Celebrating the 62nd Anniversary of the Founding of the People’s Republic of China in Sheraton Hotel, Istanbul, on September 19, 2011 and gave the following speech:

Your Excellency Mr. Hüseyin Avni MUTLU, Governor of Istanbul,

Distinguished Colleagues and madams of the Consular Corps,

Dear oversea Chinese and fellow citizens,

Guests and friends,

Ladies and Gentlemen,

Good evening! It is the great honor for me and my wife to hold the reception here this evening to celebrate the 62nd anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic of China. On behalf of the Consulate General and also in the name of my wife and myself, I would like to extend our warm welcome to all the guests here and express festive greetings to all oversea Chinese in duty area of the consulate.

Ladies and gentlemen,

In the past six decades and more since the founding of the People’s Republic of China, particularly since the introduction of reform and opening-up policies in 1978, through arduous struggle and hard working, the Chinese people have succeeded in finding a path of development conforming to China’s reality. That is the path of socialism with Chinese characteristics. Through this path, China has made remarkable achievements in many aspects. China’s overall strength has grown considerably. Its total economic output reached 5.88 trillion US Dollar in 2010, over 16 times that of 1978, rising to 9.3 percent of the world’s total economy from 1.8 percent in 1978. The country’s total import and export volume grew form 20.6 billion US Dollars in 1978 to 2.974 trillion US Dollars in 2010. Utilized foreign direct investment from 1979 to 2010 totaled 1.04838 trillion US Dollars. Economic development of China has created over 14 million jobs for the relevant countries and regions, and has made important contribution to the stable development of the world economy. China has contributed over 10 percent to world economic growth every year in recent years.

For China, the most populous developing country, to run itself well is the most important fulfillment of its international responsibility. As a responsible member of the international community, China abides by international law and the generally recognized principles governing international relations, and eagerly fulfills its international responsibility. For its part, China has made increasing contribution to promote world peace and development and will assume more international responsibility as its comprehensive strength increases.

At the same time, we have a clear awareness that China has a large population yet a weak economic base. It has to feed close to 20 percent of the world's population with 7.9 percent of the world's farmland and 6.5 percent of the world's fresh water. What has been achieved in its social and economic development must meet the need of 1.3 billion people, which presents a great challenge to China. The standard of living of the Chinese is not high, and China's social security system is inadequate, lagging far behind those of the developed countries. China’s modernization will be a long-term process. China will remain a developing country for a long time to come. Therefore, peaceful development is a strategic choice made by China to develop itself and make more contribution to maintain peace and prosperity of the whole world. China will unswervingly follow the path of peaceful development to promote development and harmony domestically and pursue cooperation and peace internationally.

Ladies and gentlemen,

The friendly relationship between China and Turkey runs a long history. In recent years, through the joint efforts from the leadership of both sides, China and Turkey has well established the Sino-Turkish Strategic Cooperative Relationship. The frequent exchange visits of high-level officials have witnessed the strengthened bilateral understanding. With a closer political relation, bilateral cooperation in other fields also increased rapidly, especially in the trade aspect. According to the statistics of Chinese side, the bilateral trade volume of China and Turkey has hit a record of over 15.1 billion US Dollars in the year of 2010, nearly 50 percent more than 2009. In the first half of this year, the bilateral trade volume has already reached to 9.4 billion US Dollars with a 30.7 percent increase compared with the same period of last year. China becomes the third biggest trade partner of Turkey. We have firm confidence to reach the goal set by the leaders of both sides to triple the trade volume in the next 4 years. The economic cooperation of China and Turkey contains extensive range of shipping, aerial transportation, finance, telecommunication, automobiles, machinery, steel, electricity, and mines with a promising future.

On the occasion of the 40th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relationship between China and Turkey, we have witnessed a robust momentum of the development of bilateral relation between the two countries. Next year we are going to host “Chinese Culture Year” in Turkey while the year of 2013 is “Turkish Culture Year” in China. With the increasing exchange visits of personnel and deepening development of the bilateral relation of the two countries, it becomes the common wish of both governments to maintain peaceful and friendly cooperation to meet the fundamental interests of their people. I would like to take this opportunity to express my gratefulness to everyone here and each and every friend, colleague who has supported the work of our Consulate and the development of Sino-Turkish relationship ever since a long time ago.

Guests and friends,

Thank you for attending this reception. Now may I propose a toast,

to the friendly cooperation and prosperity of China and Turkey,

to the long-standing friendship and happiness of the people of China and Turkey,

and to the health and wealth of everyone here.

Cheers!

Thank you!
