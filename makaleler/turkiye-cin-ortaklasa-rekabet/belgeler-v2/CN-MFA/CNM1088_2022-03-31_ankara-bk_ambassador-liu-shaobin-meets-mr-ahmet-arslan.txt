Ambassador Liu Shaobin meets Mr. Ahmet Arslan Chairman of the Turkey-China Interparliamentary Friendship Group of the Grand National Assembly of Turkey

On March 24, 2022, Ambassador Liu Shaobin met with Chairman of the Turkey-China Interparliamentary Friendship Group of the Grand National Assembly of Turkey.

Mr. Arslan, expressed deep condolences over the crash of a China Eastern Airlines passenger plane and extended sincere sympathy to the Chinese people on behalf of the Turkey-China Interparliamentary Friendship Group. Ambassador Liu Shaobin expressed his gratitude for this.

Ambassador Liu Shaobin said that China-Turkey relations are facing new historical opportunities under the current international situation. The exchanges between the legislative bodies constitute an important part of the bilateral relations. The two sides should further strengthen political mutual trust, deepen cooperation in various fields and enhance the friendship between the two peoples.

Mr. Arslan congratulated the Chinese side on the complete success of Beijing Winter Olympics. He stressed that Turkey and China have a long history of friendship and both are major countries shouldering important international responsibilities. The current international situation has added even more special significance to the relationship between the two countries. The Turkey-China Interparliamentary Friendship Group of the Grand National Assembly of Turkey is willing to further strengthen exchanges and cooperation with its Chinese counterpart and contribution to the further development of bilateral relations.
