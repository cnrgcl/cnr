Notice on the Application of Electronic Health Declaration Form for Foreign Passengers

Starting from 16 November,2020, all foreign passengers who are flying from Turkey to China will be required to present the Electronic Health

Declaration Form for boarding.

Foreign passengers with certificates of negative results of both nucleic acid and IgM antibody tests taken in the medical institution designated or

recognized by the Chinese Embassy in Turkey/ Chinese Consulate General in Istanbul shall apply for the Electronic Health Declaration Form by

logging on the website of http://hrhk.cs.mfa.gov.cn/H5/ via computer or smart phone, or by scanning the QR Code below.

Foreign passengers need to register, fill in the information, declare the health status and upload the testing certificates online, which will then be

examined and verified by the Chinese Embassy in Turkey/Chinese Consulate General in Istanbul. Upon approval, applicants will receive the Electronic

Health Declaration Form, which will be displayed in QR code with the "HDC" mark. Foreign passengers are required to present the electronic or

printed QR code with the "HDC" mark, and follow the procedures for inspection by relevant airlines during boarding.

Chinese passengers(including the Hong Kong SAR, the Macao SAR and the Taiwan region) can still apply for the green health code with the"HS"mark

via the WeChat Health Code app.
