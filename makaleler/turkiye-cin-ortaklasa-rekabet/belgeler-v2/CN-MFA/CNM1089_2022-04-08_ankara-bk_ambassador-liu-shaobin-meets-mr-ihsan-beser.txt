Ambassador Liu Shaobin meets with Mr. İhsan Beşer, Chairman of the Turkey-China Business Development and Support Association

On April 7, 2022, Ambassador Liu Shaobin met with Mr. İhsan Beşer, Chairman of the Turkey-China Business Development and Support Association. They exchanged views on economic, trade and cultural cooperation and exchanges between the two countries.

Ambassador Liu Shaobin spoke highly of the positive role played by the association in promoting China-Turkey relations over the years, especially the rich and colorful activities organized last year to commemorate the 50th anniversary of diplomatic relations between China and Turkey. It is hoped that the association, with its commitment to China-Turkey friendship, will make greater contributions to friendly cooperation between the two countries. Ambassador Liu expressed his appreciation for the TÜRKIYE & CHINA magazine, published by the association earlier this year and expressed the hope that the magazine will play a comprehensive and positive role in messaging friendship and cooperation between China and Turkey.

Mr. İhsan said that as a friendship group for Turkey and China, the very purpose of the association is to deepen friendship between the two countries and promote practical cooperation. Such commitment will remain unchanged. Since its inception, TÜRKIYE & CHINA has attracted wide attention from all walks of life in Turkey. The association will put great efforts into the magazine, making it an important platform for better mutual understanding between Turkey and China.

In order to promote exchanges and cooperation between China and Turkey and enhance mutual understanding, the Turkey-China Business Development and Support Association launched the magazine TÜRKIYE & CHINA in early 2022.  Printed in both Chinese and Turkish, the magazine focuses on political, economic, trade and cultural exchanges and cooperation between the two countries.
