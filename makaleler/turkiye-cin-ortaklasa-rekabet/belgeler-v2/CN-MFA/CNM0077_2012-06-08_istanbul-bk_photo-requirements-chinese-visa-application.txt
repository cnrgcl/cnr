Photo Requirements for Chinese Visa Application

From June 11th 2012, the Consular Section of Consulate General of People's Republic of China in Istanbul will implement new photo requirements for visa application. New VISA APPLICATION FORM will also come into effect. If the right photo or application form were not submitted by the applicant, the visa application may be denied. Attached is the requirements for the photo.

The applicant can download the new APPLICATION FORM from here.
