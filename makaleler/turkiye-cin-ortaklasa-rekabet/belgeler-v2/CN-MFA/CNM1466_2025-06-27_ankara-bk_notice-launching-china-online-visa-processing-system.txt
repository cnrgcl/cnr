Notice on Launching the China Online Visa Processing System

China Online Visa Processing System will be officially launched by the Chinese Embassy in Türkiye on June 30, 2025, replacing the Chinese Online Visa Application (COVA) Filling  System. Applicants could enjoy convenience of filling out the form and uploading materials online by visiting https://consular.mfa.gov.cn/VISA/. Welcome to visit the website for more details.
From June 30, 2025, the Chinese Online Visa Application (COVA) Filling System will be out of service.
