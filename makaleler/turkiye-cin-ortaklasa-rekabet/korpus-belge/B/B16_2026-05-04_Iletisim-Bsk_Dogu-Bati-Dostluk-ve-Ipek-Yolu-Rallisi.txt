President Erdoğan sees off East-West Friendship and Silk Road Rally participants

President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan bade farewell to the group participating in the East-West Friendship and Silk Road Rally.

The group participating in the East-West Friendship and Silk Road Rally, which set out from Germany on April 27 with the contributions of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs’ Directorate for EU Affairs, the Ministry of Culture and Tourism’s Türkiye Tourism Promotion and Development Agency, and TÜVTÜRK, have arrived in Ankara.

President Erdoğan met with the rally participants waiting for him with their vehicles on the street in front of the Presidential Complex.

Briefed by the East-West Friendship and Silk Road Rally Association President Nadir Serin about the rally itinerary, President Erdoğan posed for a photo with the rally participants and their vehicles in front of the July 15 Martyrs' Monument outside the Presidential Complex.

Minister of Youth and Sports Osman Aşkın Bak was also present at the meeting.
